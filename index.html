// The Problem

Every existing detection method has a critical gap

There is no cheap, always-on, autonomous system that watches for fires before they become uncontrollable. By the time a fire is confirmed, it has already grown.

Satellites

Global coverage, but passes every 6–12 hours at low resolution, with no real-time alerting.

Camera Towers

Continuous monitoring, but fixed coverage radius and requires dense deployment with direct line of sight.

Manned Aircraft

Flexible and high-resolution, but grounded in poor weather, not available 24/7, and reactive rather than proactive.

// The Plan

Where FireWatch is headed

The prototype proves detection works. The roadmap moves from watching a fire to acting on one.

Phase 1

Detect

A drone-mounted thermal camera scans continuously and streams live heat data over a private long-range radio link to a ground dashboard — no internet, no cellular, no cloud dependency.

● Working Prototype
Phase 2

Patrol Autonomously

Remove the human pilot. GPS-tagged fire reporting, autonomous flight paths, and a fleet of drones patrolling a defined territory around the clock — reporting position and status back to the ground station.

○ Next Up
Phase 3

Strike Early Ignitions

A tube-launched, folding-wing swarm deploys from a mountaintop mothership station. Each unit glides to an assigned hotspot, refines its aimpoint with an onboard thermal seeker, and delivers fire suppressant — knocking down spot fires in the first critical minutes.

○ In Design
// Current Prototype

How it works today

The entire pipeline is self-contained. Both devices auto-connect and start streaming on boot — no manual setup, no cloud, no internet.

01

Thermal Camera

Reads 768 temperature points 4× per second. Detects heat invisible to the human eye.

02

Drone Processor

Onboard Pi processes the stream and transmits over a private radio link.

03

HaLow Radio Link

900 MHz link carries data kilometers with no cellular coverage needed.

04

Ground Station

Second Pi hosts a live web dashboard accessible from any browser.

05

Live Alert

Operator is alerted instantly when temperature exceeds threshold.
