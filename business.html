Business Model

Built for the agencies that fight fires

FireWatch is designed for local, state, and national governments — the fire and land-management agencies responsible for protecting wildland.

It isn't a consumer product, though in principle it could cover rural communities far from help. We're pre-revenue today; this is how the model is built to work.

One-Time

Setup & Installation

Standing up coverage for a region: mothership drones, tower sensors, and mountaintop launchers — either integrated into existing fire watch towers or deployed as our own autonomous ones.

Annual

System & Software

A yearly fee to keep the system maintained and the software current, including access to the monitoring platform for local and state authorities coordinating the response.

Ongoing

Payload & Fleet Resupply

The core recurring cost: refilling glider suppressant payloads and replacing the gliders staged across mountaintop launchers after each deployment, so the system is always ready for the next fire.
