Current Prototype

How it works today

The entire pipeline is self-contained. Both devices auto-connect and start streaming on boot — no manual setup, no cloud, no internet.

01

Thermal Camera

Reads 768 temperature points 4× per second. Detects heat invisible to the human eye.

02

Drone Processor

Onboard Pi processes the stream and transmits over a private radio link.

03

HaLow Radio Link

900 MHz link carries data kilometers with no cellular coverage needed.

04

Ground Station

Second Pi hosts a live web dashboard accessible from any browser.

05

Live Alert

Operator is alerted instantly when temperature exceeds threshold.

This prototype is the foundation for Phase 1 — the same thermal sensing, private link, and live reporting, carried by a mothership drone that flies to a fire, confirms it, and relays its exact location to the crews who can respond.
